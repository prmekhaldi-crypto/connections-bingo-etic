# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ETIC students and event participants use the platform on phones and laptops to choose and launch a
mini-game from a sixteen-case student bingo. They need to understand which cases are playable and
enter a game with one direct action.

## Product Purpose

The platform is the single entry point for sixteen independent mini-games. Success means a player
can scan the complete bingo, recognize every available challenge, and launch it without passing
through an intermediate platform screen.

## Brand Personality

Structured, energetic, and collaborative. The experience should feel like an ETIC printed campaign
adapted to an accessible interactive surface, not like a generic game marketplace.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dashboard, app-store card grid, oversized controls, decorative effects that obscure labels,
mixed visual systems, or a layout that stops reading as one 4×4 bingo board on mobile.

## Design Principles

- Keep the sixteen-case bingo recognizable as a single composition.
- Make the direct path from case to game obvious.
- Let ETIC geometry and typography create identity while labels remain readable.
- Distinguish playable and upcoming cases without relying on color alone.
- Preserve the same task and hierarchy across desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All cases are semantic links with visible keyboard focus, readable labels, sufficient text contrast,
and non-color status text. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences, and the 4×4 layout remains
usable on narrow phone screens without horizontal scrolling.
